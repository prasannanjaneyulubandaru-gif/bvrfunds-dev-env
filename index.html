🎯 How It Works

1️⃣
Entry: Sell ATM Call + ATM Put (collect premium), Buy protective hedges
2️⃣
Initial Protection: Automatic stop loss placed at your specified % above entry price
3️⃣
First Leg Hits SL: When either CE or PE hits SL, the other leg's SL is revised to its buy price
4️⃣
Trailing Begins: The remaining leg starts trailing with 6 points trail and 0.5 step size
5️⃣
Profit Protection: As price moves in your favor, SL automatically moves to lock in profits
